त्रिSHUL VANI / RESEARCH INSTRUMENTOur spectral DSP · External references labelled · No fabricated metrics

From sound to evidence.

  1. Import a WAV/FLAC recording. The source remains locked.
  2. Drag on the spectrogram to select time and frequency bounds. Numeric fields are keyboard accessible.
  3. Preview attenuation or isolation, then apply. Undo/redo replays the recipe from the source.
  4. Switch layers at the same playback position. RMS matching affects listening only.
  5. Import an external reference with an explicit alignment offset. Positive offsets trim its start.
  6. Export the selected WAV and recipe. No clean-reference scores are inferred from processed audio.

Space: play/pause · Ctrl/Cmd+Z: undo · Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z: redo.
